Unique Selling Propositions (USP)

Slide 5 / 10

1. Non-Invasive Retrofitting

Installs on existing cast-iron, ductile-iron, and HDPE pipes without digging up city roads or disrupting daily traffic.

2. Acoustic-Hydraulic Fusion

Eliminates false alarms by correlating hydraulic pressure dips with high-frequency structural acoustic noise.

3. Solar Grid Tariff Synchronization

First-of-its-kind dynamic integration with state electricity DISCOM ToD tariffs, pre-filling reservoirs when solar is abundant.

4. Turnkey Open-Source Platform

Zero vendor lock-in; easily integrates with existing SCADA systems or operates as an independent municipal pilot.
