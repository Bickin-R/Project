### Field 4: Problem Statement
**Requirement:** State the problem in your context, who is affected, and key gaps/challenges. (Max 500 characters)  
**Character Count:** 494 / 500 characters

```text
Rapid urbanization in Indian cities strains water networks. Over 40% of treated municipal water is lost to subsurface pipeline leaks and unauthorized draws before reaching citizens. Furthermore, manual valve operations and uncoordinated pump schedules cause acute distribution inequality between wards, severe pressure surges causing bursts, and high energy costs during peak grid hours. Urban poor and peripheral zones face erratic supply, while civic bodies suffer massive revenue deficit.
```

### Field 5: Solution Description
**Requirement:** Summarize approach, tech stack, key features, expected outcomes, and intended impact. (Max 500 characters)  
**Character Count:** 499 / 500 characters

```text
JalUrja AI is a full-stack digital twin & IoT platform for urban water-energy grids. It features: 1) Acoustic & pressure anomaly ML models for pinpoint leak localization, 2) Predictive demand forecasting using historical weather & consumption patterns, 3) Time-of-Day (ToD) pump scheduling optimized for off-peak solar tariffs, 4) Real-time municipal supervisor dashboard with automated alerting. Tech: FastAPI, React/Vite, Scikit-learn, WebSockets. Outcome: 35% NRW reduction and 22% energy savings.
```
